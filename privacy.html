Privacy

Dawn Press Co is a small one-woman shop in Ohio selling digital printables and spreadsheets, with a free email newsletter. Here's the whole deal on your information — short, because there isn't much to tell.

Last updated September 10, 2026

What We Collect

Your email address — only if you give it to us, by signing up for a free download or the newsletter (our email list runs on a service called Beehiiv, which stores the list for us).

Your order information — when you buy from our Etsy shop, Etsy handles the whole purchase. We see what Etsy shows every seller: your name, what you bought, and your messages to us. We never see your credit card or payment details — Etsy keeps those; they don't reach us at all.

That's it. No tracking pixels of our own, no data brokers, no buying lists.

What We Never Do

  • We never sell your information. Not your email, not your name, not anything. To anyone. Period.
  • We never share your information with other companies for their marketing.
  • We never sign you up for anything you didn't ask for.

The only people who touch your info are the services that make the shop run (Etsy for orders, Beehiiv for the newsletter) — and they have their own privacy policies covering their side.

Leaving Is Easy

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. It always works, one click, no hard feelings, no "are you sure" maze. Want your email deleted from the list entirely? Ask, and it's done.

Questions

Write us any time: hello@dawnpressco.com

Or message the shop on Etsy — a real person (the only person) answers within a day.
